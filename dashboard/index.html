Your workshares

Blocks on Quai are minted by KawPoW miners. SHA-256 and Scrypt hardware mints workshares: subsamples of hashrate that meet a lower threshold, get included in a block, and earn a share of that block's reward pool. Workshares are what make PoEM's finality work, which is why the protocol pays for them.

By lock period

rolling 12 months

Set with the password when a miner connects: no lock= keeps rewards locked two weeks at the base rate; lock=1, lock=2 and lock=3 lock them for 3, 6 or 12 months and pay more. The boost is 3.5%, 10% and 25% in the first year, decaying to 0.218%, 0.625% and 1.562% from year five.
